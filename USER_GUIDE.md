# Leafnote

A notebook, a pencil, and room to think.

Open Leafnote at the local HTTPS address provided by your administrator. Your notes save automatically as you work.

## Your first page

1. Click **Notes**, then **New note**.
2. Write on the blank paper with your mouse or stylus. **Pencil · HB** is the starting choice.
3. Click the name at the top to give your note a title.

That's it. There is no Save button to remember.

## Choose your pencil

Click the **Pencil** icon to open its options. Choose a color and a grade:

- **H** — a light, fine line.
- **HB** — an everyday pencil; the default.
- **1B** — a softer, heavier line.
- **2B** — a darker, bolder line.

These are digital pencil styles. Choose **Pen** in the same popup for a solid ink line and Fine, Medium, or Thick strokes. Leafnote remembers your writing-tool choice on this device.

Click the paper to close the popup and keep writing. Use **Eraser** to remove a stroke, or **Undo** to take back your last change. **Highlighter** adds a translucent mark.

## Prefer to type?

Click **Type (T)**, then click anywhere on the page and start typing. Its popup lets you choose **color**, **text size**, and **line spacing**.

Use Enter for a new line, spaces for spacing, and Tab to indent. To change an existing text box, click into it, open Type, and choose its formatting. Changes apply to that whole text box.

## Find your notes

Click **Notes** to see your pages and search them. Click the star beside a note's name to add it to **Favorites**. Open **Notebooks** to organize your notes.

Handwriting search needs a recognition model. Under **Search tools → Language models**, install English or experimental Marathi. Use **Review handwriting** to check the reading when needed.

## Mark up a PDF or map an idea

Open **••• → Import PDF** to bring in a document. Write, highlight, or type on its pages. Use **••• → Export** to save your annotated copy.

Choose **••• → New brainstorm board** for a roomy canvas. Add **Idea cards**, use **Connect** to join them, and use **Select** to drag them around. Double-click a card to edit its text.

## Keep your notebook safe

Notes always save in this browser on this device. If you enable **••• → Server sync**, a copy is also kept on your Linux server. Write offline as usual; reopen the app online to synchronize. First setup asks before connecting and requires your server’s connection code. Conflicting edits are kept as copies.

For a copy you can keep, open **Notes → Library actions → Export backup**. Import that backup to restore your notebook later. Importing a backup replaces the notes currently on that device.

## Recover a note

**Move to Trash** retains the note. Open **••• → Dashboard → Trash** to restore it, even offline. **Revision history** shows server autosaves when sync is connected. Select a revision and use **Restore as new note** to recover an older version while keeping the current note. Offline edits become a Git revision when they next sync.

## Find a note by its page

Leafnote opens to large page thumbnails. Click a card to write, and click **Notes** to return to the overview. **Grid** fills the window; **List** keeps your note list beside the writing page. your device remembers the choice. Search, Favorites, notebooks, and sorting work in either view. PDF thumbnails show the first page and annotations; search previews show the matching PDF page. Brainstorm thumbnails show cards and connections. **Continue writing** returns to the current note. Import PDF, new boards, backups, and Dashboard are available under **Library actions**.

## Pages and paper

Use **Add page after current** to insert a blank page where you are writing. Each page has its own Plain, Lined, Grid, or Dots choice. Scroll to older/newer pages and click a page to edit it, or use Previous/Next and the page number. PDF paper guides overlay the original; its content remains.

## Dashboard and storage

Open **Library actions ? Dashboard** in the library header. Overview shows note/document size, browser-estimated app storage, and the configured Linux sync folder when paired. Location controls guide changing server address or copying a Linux folder-change command. **Trash** offers Restore and confirmed Delete permanently. Permanent deletion removes the current record and local PDF; old Git revisions and backups can retain copies.

## Arrange pages

New notes and blank inserted pages start Lined; existing paper choices stay unchanged. Each displayed page has a numbered Add button and a **Page actions** menu. Move a page up/down or to an exact position, or choose **Delete page** and confirm. The last page is protected; move the whole note to Trash if needed. The selected page is remembered on this device. Page deletion changes the current document; older Git revisions and backups can retain it.
